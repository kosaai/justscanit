Last updated: January 10, 2026

TL;DR

We don't collect any data. Period. Your scanned documents never leave your device. There are no servers, no analytics, no tracking. The app works entirely offline.

What Data We Collect

None. Just Scan It does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data. We have no servers. We have no analytics. We have no way to access your documents or any information about you.

Device Permissions

Just Scan It requires one permission to function:

  • Camera — Required to scan documents. Images captured by the camera are processed entirely on your device and are never transmitted anywhere.

That's it. No access to contacts, location, microphone, or any other device features.

Where Your Data Is Stored

All scanned documents and app data are stored locally on your device in the app's private sandbox. This data is:

  • Accessible only to you through the app
  • Protected by your device's security (passcode, Face ID, Touch ID)
  • Automatically included in your iCloud backup if you have that enabled
  • Deleted when you uninstall the app

Note: If you enable iCloud backup on your device, your scanned documents may be included in your device backup. This is controlled by Apple and your device settings, not by our app.

Third-Party Services

Just Scan It does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools. There is no:

  • Google Analytics, Firebase, or any analytics
  • Crash reporting services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Cloud storage services

The app is completely self-contained.

Sharing & Export

When you export or share a scanned document (as PDF or image), you're using iOS's built-in sharing system. The destination of that shared file is determined by you — we have no visibility or control over where you send it.

Children's Privacy

Since we don't collect any data from anyone, we also don't collect data from children. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Open Source

Just Scan It is open source. You can verify everything stated in this policy by reviewing our source code on GitHub. We believe transparency is the foundation of trust.

Verify yourself: Our complete source code is available at github.com/kuvkir/justscanit

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. But honestly, there's not much to change — we don't collect data now, and we don't plan to start.

QUESTIONS?

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can reach us through GitHub.
